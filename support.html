Duo 麻將館 支援

港式台灣牌（十六張）麻將，支援 iPhone Duo、iPhone、iPad、Mac。

常見問題

線上對戰連不上？
請確認已連上網路，並在「設定 → Game Center」登入。和朋友玩可以用「建立房間」，按右上「邀請」複製連結傳給對方，或請對方輸入同一個房號。

換裝置後沒有進度？
兩部裝置需登入同一個 Apple ID 並開啟 iCloud。同步通常需要幾秒到半分鐘。

規則和番數在哪裡看？
主選單「規則說明」有完整規則與番數表；「教學局」有 14 課互動教學。

遊戲需要付錢嗎？
遊戲免費遊玩。分數只用於計分，不涉及真實金錢。

Duo Mahjong Support

Hong Kong–style Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong for iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Can't connect online? Make sure you're online and signed in under Settings → Game Center. To play with friends, create a room and tap Invite to copy a link, or share the room code.

Progress missing on another device? Both devices need the same Apple ID with iCloud on. Syncing can take up to half a minute.

Does the game cost money? It's free to play. Points are for scoring only; no real money is involved.
